F.R.E.T

Flexible Real-Time Effects Terminal

A digital signal processing guitar effects pedal, designed and built by a three-person team of Electrical Engineering students over two semesters.

Project overview

F.R.E.T (Flexible Real-Time Effects Terminal) is a guitar effects pedal that uses digital signal processing to apply real-time audio effects to an electric guitar signal. The pedal accepts a standard instrument-level input from an electric guitar, processes the signal digitally to apply one or more configurable effects, and outputs a single mono signal ready to feed directly into a guitar amplifier. This is a student-led engineering project, covering everything from circuit and signal-chain design through firmware, enclosure, and testing.
