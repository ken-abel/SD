About Us

One pedal.
Endless possibilities.

F.R.E.T (Flexible Real-Time Effects Terminal) is a programmable multi-effect guitar pedal designed to give musicians access to multiple effects and tone customizations within a single unit.

Instead of purchasing and connecting multiple traditional pedals, F.R.E.T combines a variety of digital effects into one flexible platform. Users can create their own signal chains and customize their sound to fit their individual playing style.

The project is designed with an open-source philosophy, allowing users and developers to experiment with the software and create their own effects and features.

The Concept

creativity.

01

Multiple Effects

Access effects such as distortion, reverb, delay, compression, and other digital effects within one platform.

02

Custom Signal Chains

Arrange and customize the order of effects to create unique signal paths and personalized tones.

03

Open Source

The software architecture is designed to allow users and developers to experiment, modify, and create new effects.

04

Real-Time Processing

Designed around real-time digital audio processing for responsive performance while playing guitar.

05

Hands-On Control

Footswitches and rotary controls provide direct access to effects, parameters, navigation, and volume.

06

Expandable Platform

F.R.E.T is designed with future hardware and software expansion in mind as the project continues to develop.
